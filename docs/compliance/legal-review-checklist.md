# Legal review checklist — PolyNurse consent phase

**Every item below is a finding requiring either implementation (done in this phase) or confirmation by a qualified Nigerian lawyer before publication.** Status key: `Fixed`, `Partially Fixed`, `Blocked`, `Needs Legal Review`.

| ID | Finding | Affected feature | Risk / impact | Standard / legal concern | Remediation | Verification | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | AdSense script loads regardless of cookie-consent choice | `/dashboard`, `/community`, `/voting` | Optional marketing tracker activated without consent | NDPA 2023 Part III (consent); NDPC GAID 2025 Art. 7(l); FCCPC Reg. 4 (no consent-by-inaction) | Gate `adsbygoogle.js` injection on `granted('marketing') === true`; consent state persists | Network tab: script absent when marketing denied | Partially Fixed (gate code added; e2e assertion pending) |
| C2 | Cookie notice is a bottom banner | Landing + all routes | NDPC GAID 2025 Art. 7(l) explicitly says bottom-only notices are insufficient; must obstruct middle/left/right | NDPC GAID 2025 Art. 7(l) | ConsentManager renders a visibly obstructing overlay, dismissible only by explicit affirmative action | Overlay present on first visit; no close-by-Escape/backdrop | Partially Fixed (design implemented) |
| C3 | No per-category consent; accept-all/decline toggle only | Cookie banner | No granular choice; marketing not separable from necessary | NDPA consent; FCCPC Reg. 4 | ConsentManager: Strictly Necessary (informational, off), Preferences, Analytics, Advertising & Marketing — all optional default OFF | Toggle states + persisted record | Fixed |
| C4 | No way to change/withdraw consent after first choice | `apex_cookie_consent` | Rejection/acceptance locked forever | NDPA right to withdraw; FCCPC Reg. 4 | Persistent "Cookie & privacy preferences" control in Settings; consent record updatable | Settings control opens manager; second choice overwrites record with new timestamp | Fixed |
| C5 | Consent record holds no version | `apex_cookie_consent` | Cannot tie a choice to a policy version; material changes not re-prompted | NDPA transparency; FCCPC Reg. 3 | New record shape `{version, timestamp, categories, source}`; version constant in `cookieManifest.js` | Record includes version; version bump triggers re-prompt (logic) | Fixed (logic); re-prompt-on-version-change wired to manifest version |
| C6 | No data-inventory / vendor list published | Legal pages | Privacy policy claims must match real data flows | NDPA 2023 Part III; FCCPC Reg. 2 | `docs/compliance/storage-manifest.md` created; legal pages to be rewritten in later phase | Manifest matches code audit | Fixed (manifest) |
| C7 | Legal business identity missing | All legal pages | "Apex Scholars (Polynurse Exam Center)" not confirmed as legal CAC entity; no address/email/DPO | NDPA controller-identity disclosure; FCCPC Reg. 13 distance contracts | `src/config/legalConfig.js` placeholders flagged; documents marked `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED` | Placeholders visible in code, never rendered as real details | Blocked (needs business input) |
| C8 | DCPMI registration status undetermined | Organisation | Whether PolyNurse must register with NDPC, designate a DPO, file CAR | NDPC GAID 2025 Art. 8–10 | Facts documented in `legal-sources-register.md`; marked for counsel | — | Needs Legal Review |
| C9 | "We never sell your data" claim | Cookie banner / Privacy | Processors (Supabase, Vercel, Paystack, Google AdSense) receive data — distinguish processing from selling | NDPA; FCCPC unfair-practices | Reworded in consent copy ("service providers only; we do not sell"); privacy page rewrite deferred | Copy audit | Fixed (copy) |
| C10 | No breach-notice commitment | Privacy | NDPC requires 72-hour notice to Commission + immediate high-risk notice to subjects | NDPC GAID 2025 Art. 7(p)–(q) | Deferred to privacy-page rewrite; operationally: incident log to be added | — | Needs Legal Review |
